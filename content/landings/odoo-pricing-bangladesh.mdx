Odoo pricing in Bangladesh has three parts. The licence: Community edition is free, Odoo Enterprise is about USD 24.90 per user per month on the Standard plan and about USD 37.40 on the Custom plan at Odoo's US list price, which is up to about BDT 3,000 to 4,700 per user per month assuming BDT 120 to 125 per USD. The implementation: community-observed ranges run from BDT 3 to 8 lakh for a small SME to BDT 25 lakh to 1 crore plus for a garments or manufacturing rollout. The running cost: hosting, support and upgrades. Odoo prices vary by region and change yearly, usually between January and April, so confirm the current figure on odoo.com/pricing before you budget.

## The three cost layers

Most "how much is Odoo" questions mix up three separate budgets. Keep them apart and the numbers become predictable.

| Layer | What it covers | Who you pay | Typical Bangladesh range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Licence | The right to use Enterprise apps, updates and Odoo support | Odoo S.A. directly, or through a partner | Free (Community) to about USD 37.40 per user per month (Enterprise Custom) |
| Implementation | Discovery, configuration, data migration, Mushak and payroll localisation, training, go-live | A partner, a freelancer or your own team | BDT 3 lakh to 1 crore plus, depending on scope |
| Running cost | Hosting, backups, monitoring, bug fixes, yearly upgrades | Hosting provider plus a support retainer | BDT 60,000 to 2.4 lakh per year for a small cloud server, plus the retainer; more for a managed service |

Everything on this page applies to Odoo 17 to 19. A new major version arrives every October, and Odoo sometimes changes plan names or inclusions with it, so check the version you will be deployed on.

## Odoo licence plans explained

Odoo currently publishes three plans. The figures below are Odoo's US list prices, approximate, in USD, per user per month, billed annually. Monthly billing costs more. Odoo prices by region, and South Asian buyers usually see a lower regional USD price on odoo.com/pricing when visiting from Bangladesh, so BDT conversions built on the US price are an upper bound.

| Plan | Approximate price | What you get | Hosting options |
|---|---|---|---|
| One App Free | Free | One app, unlimited users, Odoo Online only | Odoo Online |
| Standard | About USD 24.90 | All Enterprise apps, Odoo Online hosting included | Odoo Online only |
| Custom | About USD 37.40 | All apps, Studio, multi-company, external API, choice of hosting | Odoo Online, Odoo.sh or on-premise |
| Community | Free | Open source core apps, no Odoo support | Self-hosted only |

A detail that trips up Bangladesh buyers: on the Standard plan you cannot self-host or install custom modules. If your plan includes a Mushak 6.3 report module, a bKash integration or Bangla payroll rules, you need Custom or Community. Also, every internal user who logs in counts as a user, including a warehouse clerk who scans barcodes. Portal users (customers and suppliers who only see their own documents) are free.

Odoo offers regional pricing in some markets. Whether Bangladesh is on a localised price list changes over time, so check odoo.com/pricing from a Bangladesh connection and ask a partner what they are quoting in BDT.

## Converting to BDT: a worked example

Consider a Dhaka trading company with 12 internal users: 3 in accounts, 4 in sales, 3 in the warehouse, 2 managers. This example is hypothetical and uses an assumed rate of BDT 120 to 125 per USD.

- Standard plan: 12 users at the US list price of about USD 24.90 is about USD 299 per month, or at most about BDT 36,000 to 37,500 per month, which is up to roughly BDT 4.3 to 4.5 lakh per year.
- Custom plan: 12 users at the US list price of about USD 37.40 is about USD 449 per month, at most roughly BDT 54,000 to 56,000 per month, or up to BDT 6.5 to 6.7 lakh per year.
- Community edition: BDT 0 in licences, but you host it yourself.

Add import VAT or card conversion charges if you pay by card from Bangladesh. If a local partner invoices you in BDT, ask whether the quoted price includes 15 percent VAT, because many first-time buyers only discover the VAT line on the invoice.

## Community edition: free licence, not free ownership

Community is a complete ERP for sales, purchase, inventory, invoicing, CRM, manufacturing, project and HR basics. What it does not include in Odoo 17 to 19 is the full Accounting app (general ledger reports, bank reconciliation widget, assets), Studio, Quality, Field Service, the Odoo mobile app, and official support.

The Bangladesh community fills most gaps with OCA modules, which are free. The cost shifts from licence to people: someone has to install, test and upgrade those modules every year. A reasonable rule from local experience is that Community saves you the licence fee but adds 10 to 20 percent to implementation effort and requires a technical support arrangement. For a company with under 10 users and a capable partner this is often the cheaper route over five years. For 30 or more users, Enterprise licence savings versus maintenance effort should be modelled rather than assumed.

## Hosting costs in Bangladesh

Where the system runs affects both price and compliance.

| Option | Approximate cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Odoo Online (SaaS) | Included in Standard and Custom plans | No custom modules on Standard. Data is hosted abroad. |
| Odoo.sh | Priced per worker and storage, in USD, on top of the Custom licence; check odoo.sh for the current rate | Git-based deployment, good for partners doing custom work. Data hosted abroad. |
| Local data centre or local cloud | BDT 5,000 to 20,000 per month (BDT 60,000 to 2.4 lakh per year) for a VM sized for 10 to 50 users | Data residency inside Bangladesh, which some government and financial clients require. Check backup discipline and uptime history. |
| International cloud (DigitalOcean, AWS, Hetzner) | USD 25 to 150 per month, roughly BDT 3,000 to 19,000 | Mature tooling and snapshots. Pay in USD. Latency from Dhaka is acceptable for Singapore regions. |

Add backups, monitoring and an SSL certificate. For self-hosted Community, budget BDT 5,000 to 20,000 per month (BDT 60,000 to 2.4 lakh per year) for a small cloud server and basic management, and more if you want a managed service with a named engineer.

## Implementation: the largest number

Licence fees are usually a minority of the first-year spend. Implementation is where the money goes, and it depends on scope rather than on Odoo itself. Community-observed ranges for Bangladesh, which are not quotes:

| Scope | Users and apps | Observed range |
|---|---|---|
| Small SME | 2 to 5 users, 3 to 6 apps (Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Invoicing, CRM) | BDT 3 to 8 lakh |
| Mid-size company | 10 to 30 users, customisation, Mushak reports, payroll under the Labour Act 2006, approvals | BDT 8 to 25 lakh |
| Manufacturing or garments | MRP, Quality, bonded warehouse tracking, integrations with buyers or banks | BDT 25 lakh to 1 crore plus |

The [implementation cost page](/odoo-implementation-cost-bangladesh) breaks each range into its line items. The short version: data migration from Tally or Excel, Mushak 6.3 and 9.1 reports, payroll rules (festival bonus, provident fund, gratuity, overtime at double the basic rate) and training are the four items most often underestimated.

## Support and upgrades after go-live

Odoo publishes a major version each October and supports the three most recent versions. Enterprise customers on Odoo Online are upgraded automatically; on Odoo.sh or on-premise the upgrade is a project, often 10 to 30 percent of the original implementation cost if you have custom modules. Community users upgrade when they choose, but OCA module availability lags a few months after each release.

Plan for a support retainer. Local arrangements range from a few thousand taka per month for a freelancer on call to BDT 50,000 or more per month for a partner SLA with response times. The [pricing guide](/pricing-guide#components) lists the questions to ask about support scope.

## How to get a realistic quote

1. Count every internal user, including occasional ones. Each one is a licence seat on Enterprise.
2. List the apps you need in year one. Three to six is normal for a first phase.
3. Write down every Bangladesh-specific item: VAT and Mushak, withholding, payroll, bKash or Nagad, Bangla documents, bonded warehouse if you export.
4. Decide Community or Enterprise using the hosting and custom module constraints above.
5. Ask two or three providers for a line-item estimate against the same scope so you can compare like with like. The [partners page](/odoo-partners-bangladesh) explains how to evaluate them.

## Reducing total cost without cutting corners

- Start configuration-first. Most Bangladesh SMEs can run on standard flows with reports customised, not processes.
- Clean master data before migration. Dirty product and customer lists are the top cause of budget overrun.
- Phase the rollout. Accounting and Sales first, Manufacturing or Payroll in phase two.
- Train internal champions early so your support retainer handles bugs, not basic questions.
- Keep every customisation documented and in version control so the yearly upgrade is cheaper.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Odoo cost per user in Bangladesh?

Odoo's US list price is about USD 24.90 per user per month for the Standard plan and about USD 37.40 for the Custom plan, billed yearly. At BDT 120 to 125 per USD that is up to about BDT 3,000 to 4,700 per user per month. Odoo prices by region, buyers visiting odoo.com/pricing from Bangladesh usually see a lower regional USD price, and prices change yearly, so treat the BDT figure as an upper bound and confirm there.

### Is Odoo Community really free?

Yes. Odoo Community is open source under LGPL and costs nothing to licence. You pay for hosting, implementation and support. It lacks some Enterprise apps such as full Accounting, Studio, Quality and the mobile app, which the community often replaces with OCA modules.

### What is the One App Free plan?

Odoo's One App Free plan lets you use a single app with unlimited users at no licence cost on Odoo Online. It suits a company that only wants CRM or only Invoicing. Adding a second app moves you to the Standard plan for all users.

### What is the total first-year cost for a small company?

For a 5-user company on Community, a community-observed range is BDT 3 to 8 lakh for implementation plus BDT 5,000 to 20,000 per month (BDT 60,000 to 2.4 lakh per year) for a cloud server. On Enterprise Standard add up to roughly BDT 1.8 to 1.9 lakh per year in licences at Odoo's US list price, usually less at the regional price. These are ranges, not quotes.

### Do Bangladesh companies pay Odoo in USD?

Odoo Online and Enterprise subscriptions are usually billed in USD or EUR by card, or through a local partner who invoices in BDT. Factor in your bank's card conversion rate and any applicable VAT on imported services when budgeting.
