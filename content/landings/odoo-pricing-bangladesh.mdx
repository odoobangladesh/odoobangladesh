This page explains **Odoo pricing in Bangladesh** in a practical way. Instead of focusing on slogans, we break down the cost drivers and how to plan realistically.

## What “pricing” usually includes

- **Software**: users, apps/modules, edition (depends on your setup)
- **Implementation**: discovery, configuration, data migration, testing (UAT)
- **Customization**: only if workflows truly require it
- **Integrations**: eCommerce, payments, logistics, BI tools
- **Hosting + operations**: backups, monitoring, security, performance
- **Training**: end users + admins + internal process owners
- **Support**: post go-live stabilization and improvements roadmap

## A simple scope-based estimation approach

1. List departments and must-have workflows.
2. Map those workflows to **modules** (Accounting, Inventory, Manufacturing, etc.).
3. Identify the “hardest process” and run a proof-of-concept.
4. Decide a realistic v1 scope (90-day goal) and a phased roadmap.

## Reduce risk (and long-term cost)

- Start with configuration-first
- Clean master data early
- Invest in training and UAT scripts
- Keep customization documented and testable
