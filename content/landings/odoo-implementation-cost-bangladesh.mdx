Odoo implementation cost varies widely because it depends more on **scope and complexity** than on the software alone.

## Key cost drivers

- **Scope**: number of modules, users, departments
- **Process complexity**: approvals, exceptions, multi-warehouse, multi-company
- **Data migration**: quality of masters + history needed
- **Customization**: what can be configured vs what must be coded
- **Integrations**: payments, logistics, eCommerce, external accounting tools
- **Training**: role-based training for end users + admins
- **Testing and go-live**: UAT scripts, cutover plan, stabilization window

## How to budget safely

1. Define v1 scope (what must go live first).
2. Create a phased roadmap for remaining modules/features.
3. Use a checklist for readiness and a proof-of-concept for hardest workflows.

## Common mistakes that increase cost

- Starting customization too early
- Skipping data cleanup
- Weak UAT and training plan
- No change management ownership
