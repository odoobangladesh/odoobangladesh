Open source ERP can be a strong fit when you want flexibility, long-term control, and the ability to build internal capability.

## Common evaluation points

- Fit for your industry and workflows
- Customization needs vs standard process
- Talent availability (functional + technical)
- Total cost: implementation + training + maintenance
