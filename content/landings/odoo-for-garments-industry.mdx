Bangladesh’s garments sector often needs **traceable inventory**, **production planning**, **quality checks**, and **real-time reporting**. Odoo can be configured to support these workflows through Manufacturing, Inventory, Quality, Purchase, and Accounting.

## Typical use-cases

- Raw materials to finished goods tracking
- Work orders and production scheduling
- Costing, wastage tracking, and compliance reporting
