## Why SMEs choose Odoo

- Modular: start with Sales/Accounting, expand later
- Customizable: adapt workflows without changing the whole ERP
- Strong ecosystem for training and talent

## Practical checklist

- Define scope for the first 90 days
- Identify key reports stakeholders need
- Clean master data early (customers, products, chart of accounts)
