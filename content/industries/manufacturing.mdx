## Typical requirements

- Bills of materials that match what the floor actually consumes
- Raw-material stock that purchasing can trust
- Work orders and a way to see late operations
- Finished-goods cost that finance can explain

## Common modules

Manufacturing, Inventory, Purchase, and Accounting. Garments is a narrower case of the same pattern. See [Odoo for garments](/industries/garments) when traceability and production planning are the main problem.

## A first release

Configure one product family end to end before adding every work center. The [manufacturing training](/training/functional/manufacturing) checklist is the study path for that flow.
