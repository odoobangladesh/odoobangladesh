## Typical requirements

- A clear path for enquiries and follow-ups
- Fee invoices and receipts that finance can reconcile
- Staff records kept apart from student operations
- Internal work, such as a campus project, tracked without a separate spreadsheet

## Common modules

CRM, Accounting, HR, and Project. This is not a student-information system by default. Confirm which academic records stay outside the first release.

## A first release

Pick either admissions follow-up or fee collection as the first workflow, not both, unless the same small team owns the data. The [ERP checklist](/resources/erp-checklist) is the place to write that choice down.
