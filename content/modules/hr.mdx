## What it covers

- Employee records, departments, and job positions
- Contracts and working schedules
- Time off types and approvals
- The employee data payroll will later rely on

## Implementation notes (Bangladesh)

- Agree which fields are required before importing people data
- Separate HR approvals from payroll rules
- Name who owns joiners, movers, and leavers
