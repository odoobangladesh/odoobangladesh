## What it covers

- Salary structures and payslip inputs
- Attendance or time-off figures that change a payslip
- Posting payroll results where finance expects them
- What must be true in employee records before the first test run

## Implementation notes (Bangladesh)

- Do not start payroll until HR owns the employee master
- Confirm the legal entity’s pay period and who approves changes
- Treat statutory rules as a scoped configuration, and document every local rule you add
- Rehearse one full pay cycle before go-live, including the accounting entry
