## What it covers

- Customer invoices and vendor bills
- Journals, payment terms, taxes
- Reports for finance visibility

## Implementation notes (Bangladesh)

- Decide COA strategy (migration vs redesign)
- Clarify tax/VAT workflow and approvals
- Plan bank reconciliation and month-end process
