## What it covers

- Projects, stages, and tasks
- Timesheets, when billing depends on time
- Handoff from a won opportunity or a sales order into delivery
- The invoice that follows a milestone or a timesheet

## Implementation notes (Bangladesh)

- Decide whether you bill fixed price, time, or milestones before configuring tasks
- Keep stage names the same across teams
- Name who updates task status, and which report a manager will check weekly
