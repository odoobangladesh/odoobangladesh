This topic page is a learning checklist for functional consultants focusing on **Inventory**.

## Learning checklist

- Warehouses, locations, and picking types
- Receipts from purchase orders and deliveries from sales orders
- Reordering rules and replenishment
- Lots, serial numbers, and traceability
- How stock valuation hands off to accounting

## Practice

Walk one product from purchase receipt to delivery, then explain where the quantity and the value changed.
