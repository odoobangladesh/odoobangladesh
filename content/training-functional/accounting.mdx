Odoo Accounting functional training teaches you to configure and run the accounting application for a Bangladeshi company on Odoo 17 to 19: a chart of accounts that an NBR-registered business can live with, 15 percent VAT with Mushak 6.3 invoices, VDS and TDS withholding, a July to June fiscal year, bank and bKash reconciliation, and the month-end reports an owner or auditor asks for. It is the first topic in the functional track because every other module posts into accounting. Finish this page and you will know what the batch covers, what to prepare, and what to practise before moving to [Inventory](/training/functional/inventory).

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this topic you should be able to:

- Design and import a chart of accounts that maps to the company's existing ledger and to Bangladesh financial statement formats.
- Configure 15 percent VAT, zero-rated and exempt taxes, and the VDS and TDS withholding taxes used on supplier bills.
- Set up a fiscal year that runs July to June, with lock dates for closed months.
- Run the customer invoice to payment cycle and the vendor bill to payment cycle, including partial payments and credit notes.
- Reconcile a bank statement, a bKash merchant statement and a petty cash box.
- Produce a trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet, aged receivables and a VAT summary that an accountant can tie back to Mushak 9.1.
- Write a short UAT script for an accounting go-live.

## Prerequisites

You should be comfortable with double-entry bookkeeping and have read the [Accounting module](/modules/accounting) overview. Know what a Mushak 6.3 tax invoice looks like and why a VAT-registered buyer needs one. Basic spreadsheet skills matter because most configuration is imported from CSV. No Odoo experience is required.

## Practice exercises

Work through these with a trial database. They use hypothetical data, so change names freely.

**Exercise 1: Chittagong trading company chart of accounts.** Consider a Chittagong importer of industrial fasteners, registered for VAT, with two bank accounts, a bKash merchant account and a bonded warehouse facility. Build a chart of accounts with: current assets split into cash, bank (Islami Bank and BRAC Bank), bKash, trade receivables and advance income tax; inventory; fixed assets with accumulated depreciation; VAT current account, VDS payable and TDS payable as separate liabilities; and revenue split between local sales and re-exports. Import it from CSV. Then check the balance sheet layout and fix any account that landed in the wrong group.

**Exercise 2: Tax set with one invoice of each kind.** Create a 15 percent VAT sales tax, a 15 percent purchase tax, a zero-rated export tax and an exempt tax. Add a 5 percent VDS purchase withholding and a 3 percent TDS withholding as negative taxes on supplier bills. Issue a local sale of BDT 1,15,000 inclusive of VAT, an export sale of BDT 2,00,000 at zero rate, and book a supplier bill for BDT 50,000 of transport services with VDS and TDS deducted. Open the journal entries and confirm that the VAT, VDS and TDS liabilities are posted to the right accounts and that the supplier is owed the net amount.

**Exercise 3: Mixed-month reconciliation.** Import a bank statement CSV with 25 lines: customer receipts, a bKash settlement, bank charges, a cheque bounce and a loan instalment. Reconcile every line. Create the reconciliation models for bank charges and bKash fees so the second month takes minutes instead of an hour.

**Exercise 4: Month-end pack.** Produce the trial balance, P&L and balance sheet for the month, plus the tax report. Explain to a partner in two paragraphs why the VAT payable on the tax report may not match what the accountant will file on Mushak 9.1 (timing of input VAT claims, rebate rules, and transactions the accountant books outside Odoo).

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an accounting degree to take this topic?

No. You need to understand debits and credits, what a trial balance is, and how VAT works in Bangladesh. A commerce background or two years in an accounts department is enough.

### Which Odoo version does the training cover?

Odoo 17 to 19. The accounting workflows are stable across these versions. Odoo releases a new major version every October, so check the version your batch uses before you start.

### Does Odoo produce Mushak 6.3 and Mushak 9.1 out of the box?

No. The official l10n_bd localisation in Odoo 17 to 19 gives you a chart of accounts and VAT taxes from 0 to 15 percent, with the default set to 10 percent rather than 15, so change it after install. Mushak forms are usually added by a community module or a custom QWeb report, and the training shows you how to specify that requirement.

### Can I practise on Community edition?

Partly. Community edition has invoicing but the full accounting application, bank reconciliation and financial reports are Enterprise features in Odoo 17 to 19. Most batches use a trial Enterprise database or Odoo Online for practice.
