This topic page is a learning checklist for functional consultants focusing on **Point of Sale**.

## Learning checklist

- POS sessions, opening cash, and closing
- Products, barcodes, and pricelists at the counter
- Payment methods and the posting to accounting
- Multi-store setup and stock updates
- Cashier permissions

## Practice

Open a session, sell two products with different payment methods, close the session, and confirm stock and the accounting entry.
