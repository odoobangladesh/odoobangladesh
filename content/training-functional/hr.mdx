This topic page is a learning checklist for functional consultants focusing on **HR**. The module overview is on the [HR module](/modules/hr) page. Payroll rules are a later step.

## Learning checklist

- Employee records, departments, and job positions
- Contracts and working schedules
- Time off types and approvals
- What stays in HR versus what a payroll discussion must decide later
- Which reports a people team asks for in the first month

## Practice

Set up a small department, add two employees with different schedules, and record one approved leave without changing payroll rules yet.
