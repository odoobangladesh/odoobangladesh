This topic page is a learning checklist for functional consultants focusing on **Manufacturing**.

## Learning checklist

- Bills of materials and versions
- Work centers, routings, and work orders
- Component consumption and finished-goods receipt
- Production costing and the handoff to inventory
- What to confirm before a shop-floor go-live

## Practice

Build a two-level bill of materials, complete one work order, and check that components left stock and the finished product arrived.
