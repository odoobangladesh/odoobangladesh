For a Bangladeshi company choosing between Odoo and SAP Business One, Odoo is the lower-cost, more flexible and easier-to-staff option for most firms with 5 to 200 users, while SAP Business One is the more standardised, partner-governed option that suits companies whose owners, auditors or overseas buyers want the SAP name and a fixed, well-documented process model. Both run accounting, sales, purchase, inventory and light manufacturing well. The decision comes down to budget, how much you will customise, whether you need HR and POS inside the ERP, and which partner you can trust for ten years. This page compares Odoo 17 to 19 with SAP Business One for Bangladesh.

## Direct verdict

Pick Odoo if you want a modern web interface, a single system that also covers HR, payroll, POS, eCommerce and helpdesk, self-hosting in Bangladesh at low cost, and a wide pool of local consultants and developers. Pick SAP Business One if your business is trading or distribution with stable processes, you value SAP's financial controls and audit trail, your group or your bank wants a recognised brand on the ERP line, and you prefer a partner who takes full ownership of licensing, hosting and support.

Be precise about which SAP product you mean. SAP Business One (often called B1) is the SME product and the right comparison with Odoo. S/4HANA is the large-enterprise suite with project budgets in the crores and is rarely sensible for companies that are also looking at Odoo. Everything below refers to Business One.

## Pricing model

Odoo offers two routes. Odoo Community is free and self-hosted, covering accounting, invoicing, sales, purchase, inventory, basic MRP, CRM, projects and HR. Odoo Enterprise is a subscription per user per month, at Odoo's US list price with yearly billing roughly USD 24.90 on the Standard plan and USD 37.40 on the Custom plan, with a One App Free plan at zero cost. Odoo prices by region and regional prices change yearly, usually January to April; buyers visiting odoo.com/pricing from Bangladesh usually see a lower regional USD price, so confirm there. Assuming roughly BDT 120 to 125 per USD and the US price, 15 Standard users cost at most about BDT 45,000 to 47,000 per month, or up to roughly BDT 5.4 to 5.6 lakh per year. The [pricing guide](/pricing-guide#compare) shows how this stacks against other options.

SAP Business One is licensed per named user through SAP partners. There are three main user types: Professional (full access), Limited (role-restricted, for sales, logistics or finance tasks), and the Starter Package (a capped bundle for very small companies). You can buy perpetual licences and pay annual maintenance (a percentage of licence value) or take a subscription. SAP publishes list prices; partners in Bangladesh quote in USD or BDT and discount case by case. This page does not quote exact Business One prices because they are not verified for Bangladesh at the time of writing. What the community consistently observes is that a Professional user costs several times more per month than an Odoo Enterprise seat, that Limited users are cheaper but still well above Odoo's rate, and that the database platform (SAP HANA or SQL Server) adds its own licence and hardware cost.

Model the cost structure, not just the per-seat rate. A hypothetical Dhaka FMCG distributor with 25 users might run 8 Professional and 17 Limited seats on Business One, with perpetual licences, annual maintenance, a HANA or SQL Server licence, and a server to run it. On Odoo, the same company could run 10 Enterprise users for finance and management with 15 warehouse and sales staff on barcode and portal access, or run everything on Community at zero licence cost. Over five years the licence and maintenance line alone is typically several times higher on Business One.

Implementation costs differ sharply. SAP partners in Bangladesh field certified consultants with higher day rates and longer, more formal projects. A finance, sales, purchase and inventory rollout for 20 users is commonly proposed at BDT 20 to 50 lakh, with manufacturing and add-ons pushing well past that. Comparable Odoo projects land at BDT 8 to 25 lakh for mid-size scope; see the [implementation cost page](/odoo-implementation-cost-bangladesh) for how those ranges break down.

## Localisation for Bangladesh

SAP Business One has official localisations for many countries but not Bangladesh, so partners usually start from a neighbouring or generic localisation and configure the tax engine. Odoo S.A. does ship an official Bangladesh fiscal localisation: Odoo 17, 18 and 19 include `l10n_bd` ("Bangladesh - Accounting") with a chart of accounts of about 135 accounts, sale and purchase VAT taxes at 0, 2, 4.5, 5, 7.5, 10 and 15 percent plus zero-rated export and exempt, a Bangladesh tax report grouped by rate, and the districts as states. It does not include Mushak layouts, the 9.1 return, VDS/TDS or Bangla translations, and its default tax is 10 percent rather than 15, so the implementer fixes that after install and adds the rest from community modules or partner add-ons.

What both systems must deliver:

- 15 percent standard VAT, plus reduced, zero-rated and exempt rates mapped to item groups and services.
- Mushak 6.3 tax invoice layout printed from the sales invoice with BIN, VAT breakdown and the required statements.
- Mushak 6.1 and 6.2 purchase and sales registers, exportable for monthly filing.
- Mushak 9.1 return summary built from the registers. Since the Finance Act 2026 (FY2026-27) the return may be filed monthly or quarterly (covering three tax periods), due within 15 days of the end of the period; monthly filing remains available.
- VDS (VAT deducted at source) and TDS (tax deducted at source) on supplier bills, with certificates and separate ledgers.
- July to June fiscal year with period locking and year-end close.
- Bangla-language invoices, challans and payslips for front-line staff.

In Business One, the tax code and withholding tax framework can model VAT and VDS/TDS, and Mushak layouts are usually produced with Crystal Reports. The work is partner-specific; some Bangladesh SAP partners have reusable templates. Ask to see a Mushak 9.1 summary and a VDS certificate running on their demo system before you sign, and ask who updates the layouts when NBR changes the forms.

In Odoo, l10n_bd supplies the chart of accounts and VAT rates, and community modules and custom QWeb reports cover the rest of the same list, normally BDT 50,000 to 2 lakh of work. The [accounting module page](/modules/accounting) covers the configuration, and the [Odoo accounting in Bangladesh](/odoo-accounting-bangladesh) page walks through the NBR setup.

Payments and banking: bKash, Nagad and Rocket connectors are freelancer-built for Odoo and partner-built for Business One. Bank reconciliation with local banks is CSV-based in both because Bangladeshi banks rarely expose APIs. Business One's banking and cheque management are strong; Odoo's reconciliation is strong in Enterprise and basic in Community.

Payroll and HR: Odoo covers Bangladesh Labour Act 2006 rules (two festival bonuses, gratuity, provident fund, overtime at double the basic rate, earned leave) through the Enterprise payroll app or OCA modules. Business One has no native HR or payroll; Bangladesh partners pair it with a local payroll product or build an add-on. For a garments or manufacturing company where payroll is central, this is a real difference. See the [payroll module](/modules/payroll).

Hosting and residency: both can run on servers in Bangladesh. Business One is commonly on-premises or partner-hosted, which suits companies that must keep data local. Odoo self-hosts on a local VPS or data centre for a fraction of the hardware cost, since PostgreSQL on a modest server replaces a HANA or SQL Server platform.

## Implementation and partner ecosystem

Odoo in Bangladesh has a wide, varied ecosystem: several partners, many freelancers and regular [functional](/training/functional) and [technical](/training/technical) training batches. This gives competitive pricing and freedom to change providers, at the cost of uneven quality. The community advice is to check references, insist on written scope and run a proper UAT; the [implementation guide](/implementation-guide#uat) covers this. Day rates for experienced Odoo consultants in Dhaka sit roughly in the BDT 8,000 to 15,000 per person-day range.

SAP Business One in Bangladesh runs through a small number of SAP partners with certified consultants and formal methodology. Projects are documented, scoped and governed, and support contracts are structured. Quality is more consistent and pricing is higher. The main risk is lock-in: licences, add-ons, Crystal Reports and SDK customisations are tied to the partner, and the pool of independent Business One consultants in Bangladesh is thin. If your partner relationship sours, moving to another SAP partner is possible but slow.

Project timelines for comparable scope are similar: 10 to 16 weeks for a 15 to 25 user finance, sales, purchase and inventory rollout, and 6 to 12 months for manufacturing with integrations. Business One projects spend more time on infrastructure, database setup and add-on selection; Odoo projects spend more time testing community modules and agreeing which Enterprise apps are needed.

Functional fit by industry: for [distribution](/industries/distribution), both are strong. Business One has mature pricing, batch and serial tracking and a well-regarded inventory engine. Odoo matches on [inventory](/modules/inventory) and [purchase](/modules/purchase) and adds route-based sales, mobile barcode apps and bKash collection flows that distributors in Bangladesh ask for. For manufacturing, Business One's light production is adequate for assembly and kitting; Odoo's MRP, quality and shop floor apps go deeper for process and garments manufacturing. For retail, Odoo's POS is built in and works offline through load-shedding; Business One needs a third-party POS.

Upgrades: Odoo ships a major version every October; Enterprise customers get upgrade services and Community users rely on partners. SAP releases Business One feature packs and patch levels; upgrades are partner-run and add-ons must be certified for each release.

## Who should pick which

Pick Odoo when:

- You are an [SME](/industries/sme) or a growing distributor with 5 to 100 users and want seat cost near zero or at Odoo Enterprise rates.
- You want HR, payroll, POS, eCommerce and helpdesk in the same system without buying add-ons.
- You expect to customise after go-live and want local developers who can do it.
- You want to self-host in Bangladesh on affordable hardware.
- You run retail or manufacturing where Odoo's included apps replace Business One add-ons.

Pick SAP Business One when:

- Your processes are standard trading or distribution and you do not expect heavy customisation.
- Your group, lender or key customers expect SAP, or your parent company runs SAP and wants intercompany consistency.
- You want strong financial controls, a mature audit trail and partner-governed change management, and you will pay for it.
- You have budget for licences, maintenance and a dedicated partner relationship for the long term.
- Your team already has SAP experience from a previous employer or sister company.

A hypothetical example: a Chattogram importer of industrial chemicals with 20 users, bonded stock and letters of credit to track could fit either. If the owner wants the SAP name for bank and buyer relationships and has a budget of BDT 50 lakh plus, Business One with a good partner will serve well. If the owner wants to stay under BDT 15 lakh, add a field sales app and run payroll in the same system, Odoo is the better match.

## Migration considerations

Moving between Business One and Odoo is a re-implementation with data migration, not an upgrade. Master data migrates well in both directions: chart of accounts, business partners, items, price lists, warehouses, fixed assets and opening balances. Migrate open items at cutover (unpaid invoices and bills, open sales and purchase orders, stock on hand by warehouse and batch) and keep historical transactions in a read-only archive of the old system.

Plan the cutover for 1 July so the new system owns a full NBR fiscal year and the Mushak 9.1 returns do not straddle two databases. Before shutting down the old system, export the Mushak 6.1 and 6.2 registers, VDS and TDS ledgers and fixed asset schedules, because audits can look back several years.

Customisations do not transfer. Business One SDK add-ons and Crystal Reports become Odoo modules and QWeb reports; Odoo automated actions become Business One workflows or add-ons. Treat each as new work and prioritise by what users actually run each month.

Licences: moving from Business One to Odoo means stopping maintenance at renewal, and any perpetual licence value is sunk. Moving from Odoo to Business One means committing to named user licences and a database platform before go-live, so model five-year cost before deciding. The [data migration section](/implementation-guide#data) of the implementation guide covers cleansing, load order and reconciliation of opening balances.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is SAP Business One the same as SAP S/4HANA?

No. SAP Business One is SAP's product for small and mid-size companies and is the one that competes with Odoo. S/4HANA is the large-enterprise suite with a far higher cost and project size. Compare Odoo with Business One unless you are a very large group.

### How is SAP Business One licensed compared with Odoo?

Business One is licensed per named user, as a perpetual licence plus annual maintenance or as a subscription, in Professional, Limited and Starter Package tiers sold through SAP partners. Odoo Community is free; Odoo Enterprise is about USD 24.90 to 37.40 per user per month at Odoo's US list price, and usually less at the regional price shown from Bangladesh. Confirm both with a local partner and on odoo.com/pricing.

### Which handles Bangladesh VAT and Mushak better?

Only Odoo ships one, and it is partial. Odoo 17 to 19 include an official l10n_bd package with a Bangladesh chart of accounts and VAT rates up to 15 percent (the default is set to 10 percent, so change it). Mushak 6.3, the 6.1 and 6.2 registers, the 9.1 return and VDS/TDS are still community modules, partner add-ons or QWeb reports in Odoo. SAP has no Bangladesh localisation; SAP partners configure Business One's tax engine and build Mushak 6.3, 6.1, 6.2 and 9.1 layouts with Crystal Reports. Ask to see a working Mushak 9.1 summary in either system before signing.

### Which costs less over five years in Bangladesh?

Odoo, in almost every scenario at the same scope. Business One carries per-user licence or maintenance fees and higher partner day rates. Odoo Community has no licence cost and Odoo Enterprise is cheaper per seat. The gap narrows only when a company needs very little customisation and already has SAP expertise in-house.

### Can I run SAP Business One on a server in Bangladesh?

Yes. Business One is commonly deployed on-premises or in a partner-hosted cloud, and many Bangladesh installations run on local servers. Odoo also self-hosts in Bangladesh. Both can satisfy data residency requirements; the difference is in licence and database costs, since Business One on HANA needs more hardware.
