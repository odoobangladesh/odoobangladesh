## Quick summary

- **Odoo**: modular, customization-friendly, strong learning ecosystem.
- **SAP Business One**: strong for standardized processes, often partner-led implementations.

## Compare for your context

- Number of users and departments
- Need for customization vs standard process
- Reporting and compliance requirements
- Talent availability and training plan
