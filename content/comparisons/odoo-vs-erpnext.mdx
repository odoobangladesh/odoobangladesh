This guide compares **Odoo** and **ERPNext** in a practical way for Bangladesh-based teams.

## Quick perspective

- Choose Odoo when you need a large ecosystem, many modules, and extensive customization options.
- Choose ERPNext when you want a simpler open-source stack with strong core ERP workflows.
