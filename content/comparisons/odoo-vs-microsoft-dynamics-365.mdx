## Quick summary

- **Odoo** can be a strong fit for modular rollouts and customization-friendly workflows.
- **Dynamics 365** may fit organizations already aligned with Microsoft ecosystems and larger enterprise processes.

## Compare on these dimensions

- Process complexity and required integrations
- Implementation timeline and change management
- Total cost: implementation + training + support
- Local talent and long-term maintainability
