## Quick checklist

- Define scope and success metrics
- Map current processes (as-is) and target process (to-be)
- Identify master data owners and cleanup plan
- Decide integrations (accounting, eCommerce, logistics, etc.)
- Set training plan for end users and admins
- Prepare UAT and go-live checklist
