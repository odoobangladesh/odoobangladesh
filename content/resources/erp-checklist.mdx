Use this checklist to plan an ERP project with fewer surprises.

## Scope & priorities

- What is your Phase 1 scope (MVP)?
- Which teams are in scope (Sales, Accounts, Warehouse, Production)?
- What reports must be available on day 1?

## Data readiness

- Master data: customers, vendors, products, chart of accounts
- Opening balances and stock
- Data cleanup and ownership

## People & process

- Executive sponsor
- Process owner per department
- Training plan and internal champions
