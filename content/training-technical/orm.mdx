This topic page is a learning checklist for developers focusing on the **ORM**.

## Learning checklist

- Models, fields, and recordsets
- `create`, `write`, `search`, and `browse`
- Computed fields and depends
- Relational fields and when an inverse matters
- How a careless search becomes a slow page

## Practice

Add a stored computed field on a custom model, search records with a domain, and explain which queries that field will trigger.
