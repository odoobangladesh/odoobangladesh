Teams often search for a fixed number, but real ERP cost is driven by **scope + complexity**.

## 1) Scope (modules and departments)

More modules and departments means more discovery, configuration, training, and testing.

## 2) Data readiness

Data cleanup is often underestimated. Products, customers, suppliers, and chart of accounts need ownership and standards.

## 3) Customization vs configuration

Configuration-first reduces long-term maintenance and upgrade risk. Customize only for workflows that truly need it.

## 4) Integrations

Payment gateways, logistics, eCommerce, and BI tools add effort and testing cycles.

## 5) Training + UAT

Training is not optional. Strong UAT scripts and role-based training reduce go-live issues.
