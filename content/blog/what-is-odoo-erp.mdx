Odoo is a modular ERP platform used to run core business workflows such as **Sales**, **Accounting**, **Inventory**, **Purchase**, **Manufacturing**, and more.

In Bangladesh, Odoo is often shortlisted because it offers:

- Flexible modules (start small, expand later)
- Strong customization ecosystem
- A large community of implementers and developers
- Options for both Community and Enterprise editions

## Who should consider Odoo in Bangladesh?

Odoo is commonly evaluated by:

- SMEs looking to standardize operations
- Retail and distribution companies needing inventory + POS
- Garments and manufacturing businesses needing MRP workflows
- Service businesses needing CRM + project management

## How to start learning Odoo

- Explore the **modules directory** to understand what Odoo can do: `/modules`
- Read the **implementation guide** to understand rollout steps: `/implementation-guide`
- Pick a track:

  - Technical: `/odoo-technical-training`
