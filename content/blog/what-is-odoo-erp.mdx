Odoo is a modular, open-source-friendly ERP platform used for sales, inventory, accounting, manufacturing, HR, and more.

## Why Odoo is popular in Bangladesh

- Flexible modules (start small, expand later)
- Strong customization ecosystem
- Fits SMEs and mid-market organizations
